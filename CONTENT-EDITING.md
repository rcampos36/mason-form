# Editing the MAISON & FORM website

Sign in at the site’s `/wp/wp-login.php` address. Editors use the shortcuts on **Dashboard → Edit the website**. Administrators also see plugins, themes, and WordPress settings. Editors do not.

Changes appear on the site after you click **Update** or **Publish**. If a page looks unchanged, reload it once.

## Pages

Under **Pages**, open Home, Work, Studio, Services, Journal, or Contact.

Each of those pages is built from **Page sections**. A section is one block on the page, such as Hero, Introduction, or Call to action. The section name is the label on its panel.

The Journal screen under Pages is the introduction at the top of the article list. The articles themselves are under **Posts**.

## Change homepage text

1. Open **Pages → Home**.
2. Open **Page content**. Each tab is one block already on the page, starting with the Hero.
3. Change the description, photograph, caption, or buttons. The large title is not the Hero heading. Edit it under **Site Settings → General Information → Company name**. Use an ampersand with spaces, such as `Maison & Form`, to split the name onto two lines.
4. Click **Update**.

Other pages use the same **Page content** panel. The page title at the top of the screen is still the WordPress title.

Adding, removing, or reordering whole sections needs the licensed ACF PRO editor. Until that is installed, the blocks already on the page stay in place and can be edited here. A blank item at the end of a list adds one card, quote, or step. Removing an item from a list needs the licensed editor.

## Update images

1. Open the page, project, or article that shows the image.
2. For a picture inside a section, use that section’s image field in **Page content**. For the main picture on a project or article, use **Featured image**. Project galleries and floor plans are under **Project images**.
3. Choose an image from the Media library, or upload one. A preview appears after you select it. Clear a gallery image to remove it, or use the empty field to add one. Update, then add another if you need more.
4. Click **Update**.

Recommended sizes:

- Hero background: 2400×1600 pixels
- Section images and galleries: 1600×1000 pixels
- Logos: PNG or WebP, at least 480 pixels wide

Wide photographs should be JPG or WebP. Set the image’s **Alt text** in the Media library so screen readers can name it. Gallery captions use the caption saved on the image.

## Add or remove sections

On a page or project, **Page content** edits the text, images, and buttons already on the page. **Page sections** is the ACF PRO control for adding, removing, and reordering those blocks. If that panel is only a note, the licensed plugin is not active yet. Content that is already on the page stays in place and remains editable under **Page content**.

When the panel is available:

- **Add section** inserts a block. Choose the type, such as Hero or Testimonials, then fill in its fields.
- Drag a section by its handle to change the order.
- **Duplicate** copies a section.
- **Remove** deletes that section from the page. It does not delete projects, articles, or media.

Click **Update** when the order looks right.

Background, alignment, and column choices are limited to the options in each dropdown.

## Add a portfolio project

1. Open **Projects → Add project**.
2. Enter the title. The title becomes the link visitors see.
3. Write the description in the main content area. A short **Excerpt** is the line on the homepage picture.
4. Set the **Featured image**.
5. Fill in **Client**, **Year**, **Location**, **Area**, **Type**, and, if there is a live site, **Website**. Leave **Type** blank to use the project category.
6. Choose **Project categories** and any related **Services**. Drag the services into the order you want.
7. Click **Publish**.

Extra gallery images and floor plans are edited under **Project images**. Additional page sections use the licensed section editor described above. The featured image, description, client, year, location, area, type, and website are edited on the project itself.

The project appears on the Work page. Featuring it inside a section on Home or Services uses the licensed section editor.

## Edit services

1. Open **Services** in the dashboard. These are the service records, not the Services page.
2. Update the title, main text, excerpt, and featured image. On the homepage the excerpt is the short line under the stage name, and the main text is the paragraph that opens from that row.
3. Fill **Deliverables**, **Duration**, and **Team**. These are the columns on the homepage stage list.
4. Use **Order** on the service to set the fallback order.
5. Click **Update**.

The Services page shows these records. **Order** sets the sequence. Choosing a different set of services inside a page section uses the licensed section editor.

## Publish a journal article

1. Open **Posts → Add post**.
2. Enter the title and the article.
3. Set the **Excerpt**, **Featured image**, and **Categories**.
4. Optionally choose **Related articles**. If you leave that empty, the article shows others from the same category.
5. Click **Publish**.

To feature an article on the journal listing, edit **Pages → Journal** and add it under **Featured articles**. Featured articles stay out of the main grid on the first page.

The homepage journal is a page section. Its heading and description are edited on **Home**. The articles in the stack are the posts chosen for that section. Each article’s title, excerpt, date, category, and featured image are edited on the post.

## Change navigation links

1. Open **Navigation**.
2. Choose **Primary** for the header, or **Footer** for the footer.
3. Add a page from the left, drag items into order, or expand an item to rename it.
4. Click **Save Menu**.

The names and order on the site come from these menus. Renaming an item here does not rename the page.

## Update contact information

1. Open **Site Settings**.
2. Open **General Information**.
3. Edit **Email**, **Phone number**, or **Address** under **Contact details**.
4. Click **Update**.

The contact page uses these details. The footer uses them too, unless the Footer tab has its own office email, phone, or address.

## Edit the header and footer

Open **Site Settings** and use the tabs:

- **General Information** — company name, business description, logos, and contact details. The company name is the large homepage title.
- **Header** — header logo, header mark, announcement bar, header button, and sticky header
- **Footer** — contact label, index, heading, introduction, studio description, column headings, office details, and the footer button. Page links are the Footer menu. Social links are on the Social Media tab.
- **Social Media** — profile URLs
- **Design Options** — accent color, header, footer, and background styles, and Motion. Motion reveals headings, photographs, and section lines. Turning it off leaves the content in place.

The announcement text and link appear only when the announcement bar is enabled. Leave copyright blank to show the current year and company name.

Header and footer links are edited under **Navigation**, not in these fields. The header and footer buttons are the optional buttons set on the Header and Footer tabs.
